<Header>IN-GAME SERVER RULES</>
<text color="#FF4D4D" fontface="Bold">Breaking these rules may result in a Kick or Permanent Ban.</>
We want this server to be competitive, chaotic, and fun without becoming miserable for everyone involved.
Use common sense, respect the community, and don't be a shit-head.

<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#E8A33D">NO CHEATING &amp; EXPLOITS</>
<Italic>This shouldn't even have to be stated.</>
• No cheating, hacking, or exploiting bugs/glitches to gain an unfair advantage.
• Do not intentionally abuse any known exploits.
• Do not joke about cheating or exploiting. We take these reports seriously and will kick or ban when necessary.
• Bulkhead is actively working to identify and remove cheaters. Help them by reporting suspicious players in game.
<text color="#7FD6FF" fontface="Bold">How to report a player:</> Tab → Scoreboard → Right Click Player → Report
Bulkhead handles investigations and enforcement regarding cheating.
Server admins reserve the right to remove or ban players we reasonably believe are cheating or exploiting.
This protects the integrity of the server and keeps accusations or exploit abuse from putting the server at risk.

<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#E8A33D">NO GRIEFING</>
Do not intentionally do things designed solely to stop other players from playing the game. This includes:

<Bold>Ramming Vehicles</>
• Do not intentionally ram helicopters or vehicles inside your own HQ.
• Do not intentionally cause friendly vehicles to crash, explode, or otherwise become unusable.
• Do not fly/drive vehicles from HQ with the sole intent of ramming enemy vehicles to destroy them.

<Bold>Destroying Friendly Crates</>
• Do not destroy player-placed crates by the vendor.
• We understand players may have different opinions about this. It does not give you permission to destroy them.

<Bold>HQ Camping</>
• Do not camp outside the enemy faction HQ with vehicles in a way that prevents players from leaving their base.
• Do not set up anti-air FOBs right outside an enemy HQ to repeatedly kill players as they leave.
• Anti-Air and fighting in No Man's Land is fine, but keep a reasonable distance from the enemy HQ.
Give players ample opportunity to leave their base and reach the Combat Zone.
We all love a good heli dogfight and hunting artillery.
Chasing someone all the way back to their HQ and then sitting outside waiting for them to leave is not that.

<Bold>Repeated Team Killing</>
• Repeated or intentional team killing will not be tolerated.
• Accidental team kills happen. If you accidentally TK someone, own it and move on.

<Bold>Kill Farming</>
• Do not intentionally revive an enemy player solely to kill them again repeatedly.
<Italic>Exhibit A: Revive → Kill → Revive → Kill</>

<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#E8A33D">RESPECTFUL CHAT &amp; VOIP</>
Friendly banter and competition between teammates &amp; factions is fine.
Being an asshole for the sake of being an asshole is not.
• Rage baiting and excessive harassment is not welcome.
• Do not intentionally dogpile, bully, or repeatedly target another player through chat or VOIP.
• Do not harass players because of their skill level or playstyle.
• <text color="#FF4D4D" fontface="Bold">Absolutely no racism, slurs, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.</>
These rules apply to All Chat, Team Chat, and VOIP.
If someone is being inappropriate, don't turn it into a public argument.
Report them in <text color="#7FD6FF">#support-or-report</> on Discord and let the admins handle it.
If no admin is online, please provide screenshots, clips, or other evidence whenever possible.

<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#E8A33D">NO STREAM SNIPING</>
<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#E8A33D">NO ADMIN IMPERSONATING</>

<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#E8A33D">ADMIN ACTIONS</>
Admins may issue: <Bold>Warnings</> • <Bold>Kicks</> • <Bold>Temporary bans</> • <Bold>Permanent bans</>
A kick is a warning, not an invitation to immediately rejoin and continue the behavior. Take the hint.
<text color="#FF4D4D" fontface="Bold">Rejoining and continuing to cause problems will result in a permanent ban.</>
Admins reserve the right to act on anything that breaks the spirit of these rules, even if it isn't explicitly listed.

<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#E8A33D">REPORTING PLAYERS</>
If you believe someone is breaking the rules, please use <text color="#7FD6FF">#support-or-report</> on Discord.
When possible, include: player name, what happened, screenshots, video clips, and any other relevant info.
There is typically an admin available while the server is active, and we review reports as quickly as possible.
Please do not start witch hunts, publicly accuse players, or try to enforce the rules yourself. Let the admins handle it.

<text size="18" fontface="Bold" color="#6BE38A">HAVE FUN!</>
At the end of the day, we're all here to play WARDOGS, shoot at each other, blow things up, and have a good time.
Our community is growing, and we'd love for you to continue being a part of it.
<Bold>Play hard. Talk your shit. Have fun. Just don't ruin the game for everyone else.</>
